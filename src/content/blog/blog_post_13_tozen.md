# Tözen — Channeling True Source Directly

Before the planes, before the gods, before Ertene's circling watch, there was the **True Source** — endless primordial water, the substrate everything else is made from. Every event in every plane is True Source rearranged into a temporary shape. Magic, matter, time, soul, thought: all of it is the same stuff wearing different clothes.

Almost nobody touches it directly. A Tamga inscriber binds a sliver of it into a rune. A Sülde bender petitions an Iye for a fragment. An Outsource practitioner borrows it from a plane holding it in trust. An Alchemist consumes it slowly, processed through a Path. Every one of those is mediated — the Source passes through something before it arrives.

A **Tözen** doesn't mediate. They grasp the field and weave with it bare-handed, with nothing between them and the bottom of the universe. That is a godly act in the literal sense, and almost everyone who can do it is a god, a demigod, an Illumined Monarch, or a plane owner. It isn't a class and it isn't leveled. It's a capacity layered onto whatever a being already is.

The cosmology underneath it is a symmetry I'm fond of. The universe runs as two halves of one coin: **Oryan**, the flow and motion and change, and **Nogan**, the stillness and return and ground. Neither sits above the other. A Tözen on one side perceives their own current as pure and the other side's as inverted and wrong. There is no objective bright half; each side is convinced theirs is it, and both are right from where they stand.

Mechanically the currents are identical, with one consequence: bring equal weaves from opposite sides into contact and they annihilate completely. No residue, no echo. It's the only thing that can fully unmake a True Source working, and it requires someone from the other side of creation to do it.

Channeling starts with **Kavrayış**, the Embrace — opening yourself and letting the Source flow in. The check to get in is easy; the design is entirely about what holding it costs. A Tözen can stand in the Embrace doing nothing, walk around in it, hold it indefinitely. What they cannot do is rest in it. No sleep, no natural healing, no fatigue recovery. The Source running through a living body precludes the body's ordinary maintenance, so the question stops being how much power you have and becomes how long you'll go without repair.

The other thing the Embrace costs is privacy. While embraced, a Tözen radiates a **Kaynak İzi** — a source-signature, sixty feet in every direction, visible and audible and felt. It has no fixed form; it's shaped by who the Tözen is. A light or a darkness, a ringing or total silence, ozone or blood or fresh water, the feeling of being watched. Some are a truth-aura where nobody in range can deliberately lie. A serene ascended philosopher and a wrathful war-god reach into the same field and look nothing alike doing it.

You cannot suppress it. Individual weaves can be inverted and hidden; the Embrace itself is naked. Every Tamga practitioner within sixty feet knows channeling is happening, and every divine-aligned being knows regardless of distance. Tözen who need to work covertly have to be brief or work through intermediaries, and that constraint has generated more plot for me than the power ever has.

The resource is **Cürek**, and its formula says what the system is about: primary ability score, times character level, times Tözen Tier. Tier runs one to five and tracks what you are _as a channeler_, independently of what you are in the world. A recently awakened commoner sits around 36 points; an apex plane owner is near ten thousand. Recovery scales too, and not in the players' favour, which is why most plane owners experience a long rest as roughly an afternoon.

All of which would make Tözen purely a way of statting the top of the world, except for the exception. Roughly one mortal in a billion is born able to grasp the Source. Almost all of them die the first time they accidentally do, because the field is too vast for an undivine mind to hold. The handful who survive survive because something about their body or circumstance happened to provide a structural anchor — and that anchor becomes their **Engel**.

An Engel is a condition for channeling. A wilder can reach the Source only while the condition is met, and if it isn't, the Embrace simply fails no matter how much Cürek they have. It might be an emotional state — only while genuinely furious, only while perfectly calm, only when another person's life depends on it. It might be a physical object with nothing magical about it, a pendant from a grandmother, which permanently disables them if lost. It might be an environment, a substance, an action, a time of day, or the presence of one specific person or tree. The rarest wilders have several stacked, and when their conditions align they can do things matching channelers several tiers above them.

The Engel is the reason wilders are the best thing in this system. It converts a limitation into a character. A wilder who can only channel in mortal danger is a fundamentally different person from one who can only channel while still, and neither chose it. It also carries a trap I like: the Engel grants a bigger pool and a free competence while it holds, but a wilder cannot advance past Tier 1 while it's in place. Getting stronger means giving up the thing that lets you do it at all.

The tradeoff is that Tözen isn't really available to a normal party, and I've made peace with that. It's the layer the world's actual powers operate on, written down so their capabilities are consistent rather than improvised. The wilder is the one door in — and it's a door that costs a character their ordinary life to walk through.
